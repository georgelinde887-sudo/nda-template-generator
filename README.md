---

## Overview

Startups regularly enter discussions with prospective investors, strategic acquirers, and corporate partners. Standard boilerplate NDAs can be one-sided or overly restrictive.

The **NDA Template Generator** enables founders and counsel to quickly generate clean, standardized confidentiality agreements:
- **Mutual & Unilateral Modes:** Switch between two-way reciprocal agreements and one-way disclosure agreements.
- **Venture Standard Terms:** Balanced exclusions for publicly available information, prior possession, and independent development.
- **Multiple Jurisdictions:** Delaware, California, New York, and England & Wales.
- **Export Options:** Instantly copy formatted Markdown or download `.md` files ready for counsel signature.
